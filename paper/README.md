# Paper

The paper that this repository accompanies, as submitted to the 68th Programming Symposium of the Information Processing Society of Japan (IPSJ), with its figures.

- 中山 心太「盤面と正解手順を同時に生成する詰将棋の自動作問：MaskGIT と厳密ソルバによる強化学習」第68回プログラミング・シンポジウム（2027年1月8〜10日）予稿、投稿版。
- Shinta Nakayama, "Automatic Tsume-Shogi Composition by Jointly Generating the Board and the Solution: Reinforcement Learning with MaskGIT and an Exact Solver," 68th Programming Symposium (IPSJ), January 8–10, 2027, submitted version.

The page numbers of the proceedings will be added here once they are published.
